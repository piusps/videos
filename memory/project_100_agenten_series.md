Every episode re-frames: Tag x/100 · Ziel: erster zahlender Kunde · Stand (Kunden 0) scoreboard, strong value at every moment, cliffhanger + comment mechanic (Tag 2: "Wie viele von 20 antworten?").

**Why:** Pius wants a pull toward the series outcome; Tag 2 v1 was rejected as unrealistic (too much revenue too early).
**How to apply:** realistic day-by-day progress (Tag 2 = lead research + LinkedIn outreach, Tag 3 = real replies), tasks given by tagging @Superkind in Slack/Teams, guides always also show the Claude path. Outcomes revealed next day must be real numbers, never invented. Related: [[feedback-series-storybogen]], [[feedback-outcome-first]].

**Gamification + movement (Pius, 2026-10-22, Tag 2 v5):** it felt too "high / white label" without them. Every episode has: a 100-segment day bar (past days full, today fills, tomorrow pulses), "MISSION n/3" chips ticking green with SFX, an achievement toast ("🏆 MISSION TAG x GESCHAFFT"), a wiggling "🔒 TAG x+1 schaltet morgen frei" pill at the end, and the bulb-headed SkBot walking around (walk cycle + wander path), not standing still. Keep HUD/toasts clear of monitors and boards in every camera shot.

**Town format (Pius, 2026-10-22, Tag 2 v6):** series is now "Was passiert, wenn KI 100 Tage lang mein Unternehmen komplett übernimmt?" — Pius' EXISTING company, not built from zero. Company = small town, each department = house (Positionierung, Vertrieb, Marketing, Support, Buchhaltung, Personal, Lager + Chefbüro); roofs green = KI, orange = today, grey/padlock = still human; HUD "KI-HÄUSER n/8". Tag 1 = Positionierung (website + what customers really struggle with), NOT marketing. Say "Du steuerst mit" in the hook: comment + Beruf → that person moves in as a yellow resident bot (IG handle + job) next episode. Residents only from real comments, never invented.

**Slow pacing + Bürgermeister (Pius, 2026-10-22 after Tag 2 v6):** 100 days = work SLOWLY. One episode = ONE thing, shown in detail over 30–42 s (e.g. only the lead search, outreach the next day), not several steps per day. Keep the 100-day progress bar ("mega geil"). New recurring figure: the **Bürgermeister** = the KI, who has a say in everything in the town (gives orders, decides, approves). Starts with Tag 3.
