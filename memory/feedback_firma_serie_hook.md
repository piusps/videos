Every episode of "Superkind baut eine Firma" must make sense to a viewer who never saw the others, while all episodes share one thread.
- Fixed opener, spoken and on screen: "KI baut ein Unternehmen von Null. Ganz alleine. Tag N." (SeriesHook in SuperkindFirmaTag3.tsx; HUD fades in after it).
- Then: goal + till ("Ziel: 1 Mio in 100 Tagen. In der Kasse: X") → the ONE question of the day → explained on one concrete example → a takeaway the viewer can use → comment task.
- Name things concretely; never say "das Abo" without explaining what it is.
- Never mention that nobody voted/commented, no "Gestern" recap board with 0 votes.
- Slow pace: 100 episodes to fill. Small real steps (one Abo customer = 49 €/month), no jumps of 1.000 €+.

**Why:** Pius 2026-10-23: hooks were "kacke", "keiner hat abgestimmt" is "mega wack", new viewers didn't understand "welches Abo", and 500 € after day 2 is too fast for 100 days.
**How to apply:** check every script against the standalone order above before TTS; rules also in ~/Desktop/Superkind Videos/_Serie Superkind baut Firma/wirtschaftsbuch.md (Tempo-Regel). Related: [[project-superkind-economy]], [[project-100-agenten-series]].
