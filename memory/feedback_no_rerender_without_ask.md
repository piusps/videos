I stopped v1 and re-rendered v2 (~35 credits). His reaction: "bro ich meinte eigentlich den alten render aber gut wtf".

**Why:** HeyGen credits are real money; a wording preference doesn't automatically justify a new render.

**How to apply:** when a render already exists or is running and the user asks for script/wording changes, ask "neu rendern (≈N Credits) oder alten Render behalten?" before spending. Default is to keep the existing render. Also as a rule for future scripts: FP-N scripts have no "Schritt eins/zwei/drei" and no "Und jetzt kommt der eigentliche Trick"; use a natural flow.
