Pius on FP-N "Abmahnung", 2026-10-22, in two rounds:
1. "bei den ritter szenen mal was anderes reinmachen, das passt ja nicht zu jedem video". The pixel knight arena is not a standard world.
2. The replacement dark law-office desk was rejected too ("dunkle B-Roll mit ganz schlechten Karten", "sehr stock icon image behaftet"). He wants every B-roll in "unserem Superkind Weiß-Orange Branding … wirklich mit Claude-Klickpfaden … richtig clean, elegant und modern".

**Why:** dark or themed worlds break the brand and read as a template. A real click path shows the viewer exactly what to do: what to upload, where to click, what comes out.

**How to apply:**
- Full-frame moments stay light. Use an off-white grid, an orange glow, white cards and an orange § tile; never dark backgrounds.
- Step scenes are recreated light claude.ai click paths with a mouse pointer and click ripple:
  - composer with "+", "Opus 5.5" and the orange send button
  - "+" menu with "Add files or photos" and the other items
  - file chip, chat bubbles and the "Fetched …" chip
  - an artifact panel with "Kopieren"
- Use no coins, scales or icon clip-art.
- Reference: broll/src/opus/AbmahnungOpus.tsx v2 (Pointer, Composer, ClaudeWin, FileChip, LightBg).
- Use knights only on explicit request for fight topics. Related: [[feedback-fpn-opus-stil]], [[feedback-claude-real-ui]].
