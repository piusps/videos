Feedback on the Superkind Desk Agent v1 (2026-10-20):
- **Agent voice:** a natural, young German female ElevenLabs voice (Ava AnvlJBAqSLDzEevYr9Ap; runner-up Laura zKHQdbB8oaQ7roNTiDTK). Never "Lisa", which sounds like a grandmother.
- **Agent UI:** white with orange, Superkind branding. No dark glass.
- **Captions:** exactly 50/50 on the seam between screen and B-roll, with no logo next to agent captions.
- **Hands B-roll:** show one press of the mic/dictation key, then hands resting. No constant typing, and the press must match the click SFX.
- **SFX:** luxurious and premium (soft glass/wood ticks, no toy pops).
- **Motion:** Clicky-like. A cursor moves over the screen, the agent visibly searches in the app, there are push-ins, and nothing is ever static ("mehr Dopamin").

**Why:** the reel should feel like a premium product and keep retention high.

**How to apply:** use these as defaults for every Superkind agent/product demo reel. Related: [[project-superkind-desk]].
