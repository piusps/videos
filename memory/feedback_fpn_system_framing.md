FP-N scripts frame the method as Florent's own system/infrastructure (e.g. "dein eigenes Filmstudio in Claude", "mein Regiesystem"), and the CTA promises "mein komplettes System" in the guide, not "die Prompts" / "den Prompt".
New-feature names (e.g. Claude Motion) are only the mechanism, never the headline, because "das kennt ja schon jeder". If the hook claims an output ("dieser Werbefilm"), the real output has to be on screen in frame 1 and it has to look great.

**Why:** Pius 2026-10-29 on the Erklärfilm script. He found "das heißt Claude Motion" too thin and wanted it packaged "krasser" as a real system.

**How to apply:** Use this in video-script for every FP-N script, together with [[feedback-hook-rotation]] and [[feedback-outcome-first]]. The guide ([[feedback-guide-v3-value]]) has to actually deliver the full system.

Follow barrier (2026-10-31): every FP-N script has "Folg mir, wenn du keins meiner Systeme mehr verpassen willst." right after the hook (~5 s, never as the very first words), then "Denn dafür habe ich ein komplettes System entwickelt …". End CTA only "Kommentiere X, und ich schick es dir." (no second "folg mir").
**Why:** Pius wants follower growth on top of comments; the steuerberater/Pauschale reel took off and brought many followers.
**How to apply:** build it into every new FP-N script before the TTS probe.
