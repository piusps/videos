Since 2026-10-21 (Pius, after comparing with the "Pier 9" reference reel pair): our episodes were overloaded — too many facts, too fast, monotone main clauses. v4 of Tag 1 is the template.

**Why:** The reference has the same word count (~120–135 words) but only ONE thread; every sentence sharpens the previous one instead of opening something new. That makes people comment and come back.

**How to apply:**
- One thread per episode, max ~8 story facts; no name/logo/stat cards/feature lists.
- Choice is binary (A/B) and grows visibly out of the story; a second voice (anonymous mail etc.) tilts it into a trust question.
- Rhythm: one-word sentences, fragments, repetition with variation, direct speech, questions at peaks; avoid "Label: Wert" sentences.
- Pitching the A/B options reads like an ending (Pius, v5): put the threat/second voice BEFORE the pitch, keep the options hidden ("verdeckt") until late, pitch them in the last ~25 %, then threat line → vote → hard cut on the outro (v6 order). No resting beats like "wartet auf dich", no outro board.
- End on an open question; reveal only in the next episode. Clock hits midnight at the outro.
- No/low votes (decided 2026-10-22, Tag 1 got none): never fake numbers. 0 votes → "Keiner hat entschieden. Also hat er entschieden." – the mail sender/rival picks (payoff of "Sonst entscheiden die anderen"), outcome from the sealed economy table; 1–9 → name the real small numbers; ≥10 → "Die meisten von euch…"; tie → rival casts the deciding vote.
- Follow-up episode: one-line recap → "Die meisten von euch haben X gewählt" → consequence → twist that reinterprets the second voice → new binary choice from the twist → cliffhanger.
