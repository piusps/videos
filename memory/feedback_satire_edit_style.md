Since 2026-09-30 satire reels (ki-satire scripts) use a separate edit style modelled on help.hannaa: speaker full frame, stickers above the head on the named word, thin white serif titles, hand-drawn arrows, one-word white captions at chest height, one punch-in before the punchline, no music, no trigger word. Rules live in `~/.claude/skills/video-satire-edit/SKILL.md` (kit: broll/src/components/SatireKit.tsx).

**Why:** Pius asked "gewöhn dir für die Satire-Videos einen anderen Editing-Style an" with three help.hannaa reels as reference. He also chose to keep the avatar voice for satire; the jokes are therefore also put on screen (mockups, typed punchline) because the voice can't play irony.

**How to apply:** satire → video-satire-edit; Ersetzt-Demos/tutorials stay in the explainer style ([[feedback-explainer-broll-style]]). The old "no overlays on avatar" rule ([[feedback-autonomous-video-runs]]) does not apply to satire.
