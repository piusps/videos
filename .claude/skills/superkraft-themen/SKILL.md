# superkraft-themen

A superpower topic shows a viewer a **locked door** that is now open: they have wanted something for a long time, ruled it out for themselves because a **gatekeeper** stood in the way (money, training, years of practice, connections), and AI gets around that gatekeeper tonight. The topic sells **becoming**, not saving.

Two things decide whether it explodes or just runs fine: the wish is held by the **masses**, not a niche, and the topic rides a **wave** that people are talking about right now. A timeless niche wish (start a clothing brand, write a book) can pass every other trigger and still only reach the 0.5 % who happen to plan it.

Every approved topic is **Perfect** on all 8 triggers. One trigger at Average slows the whole chain and the reel sinks into the average.

All topic output (hooks, cards, keywords) is written in German. The audience is German-speaking consumers aged 18-35 with no interest in tech.

## The 8 triggers

Judge each trigger as a stranger scrolling in the evening. Answer the question with yes or no. **Yes = Perfect, no = Average.**

| # | Trigger | Question | Average | Perfect | Lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Daydream** | Would they tell a friend about this wish unprompted? | sensible wish (more productive, tidier) | life-level wish they had before the video: own income, better job, own product, freedom, status | ask "what for?" twice; the hook names the top level |
| T2 | **Locked door** | Is their first reaction "I can't do that"? | they knew it was possible, just tedious | they had ruled it out, and the video disproves that in seconds | name the barrier that falls ("without a line of code") |
| T3 | **Gatekeeper** | Can you name who used to get paid for this, and how much? | vague value ("saves time/money") | named professional or price that hurts: agency €5,000, developer, editor per reel, 3-year training | research the real market price and put it in the first sentence |
| T4 | **Trophy** | Can they show the result to someone tomorrow? | inner gain (knowledge, time, feeling) | something others can see or use: link, app on the phone, certificate on the profile, finished video, first customer | rephrase the promise around the visible end product |
| T5 | **Cheat code** | Does it feel like knowledge they shouldn't have? | regular tutorial ("how to use feature X") | unfair edge: free instead of paid, rebuild instead of buy, ahead of other applicants or founders | contrast with the expensive normal route or with "everyone else"; content stays legal and honest |
| T6 | **Blueprint** | Would they save the reel **and** comment the keyword? | one prompt to copy off the screen, or a path too vague to try | 5-9 visual steps, doable in one evening or weekend, too long to memorize | too small → enlarge the result into a full setup; too big → cut to the first trophy |
| T7 | **Masses** | Would most viewers say "I want that for me", not "cool for someone"? | niche project only a small share is planning right now (clothing brand, book, wedding, emigrating) | a wish most 18-35-year-olds hold or an activity they already do every week: job, money on the side, more free time, own assistant, being better than others at work or school, content, dating | swap the niche project for the mass wish underneath it ("own brand" → "money on the side"), or pick a mass activity the lever improves |
| T8 | **Wave** | Is this being talked about right now, and would it have looked different 3 months ago? | evergreen: the same reel could have run a year ago | rides a wave from the last ~4 weeks: a new AI release or capability, a viral trend, a news moment; the hook can honestly say "seit dieser Woche" or "gerade erst" | attach the mass wish to the newest lever or trend; without a dated wave the topic waits |

**A yes counts only with evidence:** one short clause saying why this exact topic meets the Perfect column. T8 additionally needs a **dated source** (release note, article, trend data, viral post) no older than ~4 weeks. Without evidence it is no. If you're torn, read `references/kalibrierung.md`.

**Approval also requires:** the lever held up in your own test; the topic is a wish (not a tool, news or a benchmark on its own); it targets private individuals; the content respects laws, terms of service and third-party rights.

## Equal rigor for every candidate

Candidate 30 gets the same scrutiny as candidate 1. To guarantee that:
- Score every candidate in the **grid** below. Fill in all 8 cells before you look at the next candidate. A cell is either `yes: <evidence>` or `no: <what is missing>`.
- Write no prose verdicts and no summary scores. Only the full grid counts.
- After step 6, run the **cold audit** (step 7). It catches the drift that creeps in towards the end.

```
Candidate | T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 | T5 | T6 | T7 | T8
```

## Flow: finding topics

1. **Find the waves.** Research what has been released, gone viral or made headlines in AI and consumer tech in the last ~4 weeks: release notes of Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and other big tools; trending GitHub projects; outlier reels on English- and German-speaking accounts; news that everyday people discuss. Record each wave with its date and source.
   *Done when:* at least as many dated waves as requested topics, none older than ~4 weeks.
2. **Match waves to mass wishes.** For each wave, ask which mass wish (T7) it suddenly makes reachable and which gatekeeper it removes. A candidate is always wave × mass wish × gatekeeper. Sources for gatekeepers: expensive professions and services, subscriptions, skills that need training, advantages only people with connections get, questions in comments.
   *Done when:* at least 3× as many candidates as requested topics, each with a dated wave, a named gatekeeper, and covering at least 4 different areas of life.
3. **Build angles and score.** 2-3 versions per candidate with a different gatekeeper, trophy or cheat code. Every angle goes through the full grid; keep the strongest per candidate.
   *Done when:* every grid row has 8 filled cells.
4. **Sharpen.** For every `no`, apply the Lift column and score the row again. A topic that still has a `no` after 3 rounds is dropped, because exactly that link would have cost the reach.
   *Done when:* every topic is either 8× yes or dropped with its weak trigger named.
5. **Test the lever.** Run the path yourself in Claude or ChatGPT (free tier preferred) and research prices and facts. Before declaring a lever impossible, try to prove yourself wrong: a second prompt, another tool, current docs.
   *Done when:* every remaining topic has a written test result and every number has a source.
6. **Hook and card.** The first sentence carries T1 and T2 (which dream, and that it used to be out of reach); where it fits naturally, it also signals the wave. Hook forms are in `references/kalibrierung.md`.
   *Done when:* every approved topic has a complete card (format below).
7. **Cold audit.** Re-score the approved cards from scratch, **in reverse order** and without looking at your earlier grid. Each card that loses a yes goes back to step 4.
   *Done when:* every approved card passes the reverse audit with 8× yes.
8. **Check the log.** `~/Documents/content-themen/log-superkraft.md`: each daydream at most once every 7 days; a hit may get a follow-up with a new angle. Append the approved cards at the bottom.
   *Done when:* the cards are in the log. If fewer topics reach 8× yes than requested, deliver fewer and say so.

## Flow: checking or sharpening one topic
Start at step 3 with the given topic (find its wave first if it has none) and work through step 7. Output the grid, the sharpening rounds, and either the finished card or the trigger it fails on.

## Flow: reviewing a posted reel
Add views, comments and date to the card in the log. If the reel is below the median of the last 10, re-score the grid in light of the real comments and name the trigger that was really Average. Write that lesson as one line under the card.
*Done when:* numbers and, for a flop, the lesson are in the log.
