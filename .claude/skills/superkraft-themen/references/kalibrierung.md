# Calibration: Average vs. Perfect

Read this when you are torn between yes and no on a trigger, or when you build a hook.

## Real data (@claudebyseb, Oct 2026, identical production on every reel)

| Reel | Views | Comments | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Claude as your own Jarvis, 9 steps, one weekend" | 91k | 1,641 | 8× yes: Iron Man daydream, "normal people can't build that", trophy = assistant on the phone, 9-step blueprint; masses = everyone wants a personal assistant; wave = agents/assistants were the topic of that week |
| "Claude court: 12 jurors vote on your idea" | 58k | 1,243 | daydream = own business; cheat code = honest verdict instead of a polite yes; trophy = verdict on your own idea |
| "PDF pages cost 3,000 tokens, free tool fixes it" | 37k | 391 | strong gatekeeper and cheat code, weak daydream → hit, not an outlier |
| "Sonnet beats Opus in a coding benchmark" | 2.4k | 11 | news without a wish: wave yes, but no daydream, no locked door, no trophy, no masses |
| "Your plan would be worth $4,500 at API prices" | 1.9k | 25 | saving instead of becoming: T1, T2, T4 are no |

## Counterexample from our own test run (2026-10-21)

"Eigene Klamottenmarke ohne Lager und Designer" passed T1-T6 and was still rejected by Pius:
- T7 no: only a small share of viewers is planning a clothing brand right now; the rest scroll on with "cool for someone".
- T8 no: print-on-demand has existed for ten years; nothing about it changed this month.
- Lift: the mass wish underneath is "money on the side"; pair it with a lever released in the last weeks, or drop it.

Same pattern for book, room makeover, world trip: real daydreams, but niche or evergreen.

## Worked example: from Average to Perfect

**Raw idea:** "Claude can edit videos now."

```
Candidate          | T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 | T5 | T6 | T7 | T8
Claude edits video | no: editing is not a dream | no: CapCut exists | no: no gatekeeper | yes: finished video | no: plain tutorial | no: one feature | yes: everyone makes content | yes only with a dated release
```

**Sharpened (German, as published):** „Cutter kosten 50 € pro Reel. So baust du dir einen KI-Cutter, der aus deinem Rohmaterial in 10 Minuten fertige Reels macht, kostenlos, in 6 Schritten.“
- T1 yes: make content and live from it without hours of editing
- T2 yes: editing counts as a professional craft
- T3 yes: editor at €50 per reel
- T4 yes: finished reels ready to post
- T5 yes: free instead of an editor
- T6 yes: 6 steps, one evening
- T7 yes: most 18-35-year-olds post content every week
- T8 yes only if the lever is new (e.g. a video feature released in the last ~4 weeks, with date and source); otherwise the topic waits

## Hook forms (output in German)

These are shapes, not templates. Each carries T1 and T2 in the first sentence.
- „Claude als dein eigener [Traumrolle].“
- „[Gatekeeper] kostet [Preis]. Das hier kostet nichts und dauert [Zeit].“
- „Du kannst nicht [Fähigkeit]? Musst du auch nicht mehr.“
- „Seit dieser Woche kann [KI] [Fähigkeit]. Heißt für dich: [Traum].“
- „[Bekannte App] kostet [Preis] im Monat. So baust du sie dir an einem Abend selbst.“
- „[KI] hat [bekannte Schwäche]. Deshalb …“ → a surprising mechanism, like the Claude court
